About this record

About this page: A record of how our development team was formed and organised. Classmates are not named: my teammate is referred to as “my teammate”, and names inside quoted messages are replaced in square brackets. The quoted messages and email are my own words.

Arranging the first meeting

Our team was spread across time zones. I proposed a time that suited both of the active members, posted it on the group board for everyone to see, and messaged each group member directly.

The team changes

Our group began with four members. One member left the course, and another did not respond to our messages or take part in the planning. My teammate and I continued as a two-person team.

Meetings

  1. 15 November 2026

    Meeting

    Team contract and splitting up the design proposal

    Discussed
    The team contract; each section of the Unit 6 design proposal; my teammate’s draft of the retriever section; whether the proposal should name a model and data sources; word counts for each section; the challenges to include.
    Decisions
    • We went through the team contract on a shared screen and both signed it during the recorded meeting. We are equal partners with no formal lead, and each of us owns named sections.
    • My teammate writes the introduction, retriever and storage sections. I write the architecture and agent communication, planner and processor sections.
    • The proposal does not commit to one model or one data source, so each of us can build our own system for the individual project.
    • The first paragraph of my teammate’s draft becomes the introduction. The retriever section describes the retriever’s design, including what happens when a search fails or returns too few results.
    • The challenges section covers working across time zones and changing meeting tools.
    • Next meeting: Sunday, 3 pm UK time.
    My part
    Write my sections in full from my point-form notes. Send my teammate a sentence on how the retriever tells the planner a search has failed. Send the signed contract to the tutor.
  2. 16 November 2026

    Email

    The signed team contract sent to the tutor

    The tutor acknowledged it the next day.

  3. 22 November 2026

    Meeting

    Checking the combined draft against the brief and rubric

    Discussed
    • My edits to the combined draft: a rewritten introduction, written as consultants addressing a client.
    • The three bulleted lists the brief asks for, which the draft did not have: requirements, assumptions and design decisions; anticipated challenges; tools.
    • Word cuts across the sections to make room for the lists, and the comments I left on my teammate’s sections.
    • Whether to name the research sources, and one reference to check.
    • The Turnitin check I ran on the draft before sending it to my teammate.
    Decisions
    • My teammate goes through each comment, accepts or adjusts it, and adds a comment saying what they changed. I then remove the comments and crossed-out text and produce the final copy.
    • “Free” comes out of the tools list, because my teammate may use a paid model.
    • My teammate checks the ReAct reference.
    • The title and both our names go at the top.
    • We submit on 28 November, ahead of the 30 November deadline, and both submit our peer evaluations at the same time. We meet once more before submitting.
    My part
    Produce the final copy, check it against the rubric again and email it to my teammate. Submit the proposal.
